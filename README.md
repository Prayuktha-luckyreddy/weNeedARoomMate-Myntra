## Overview

Seller Mentor Network is a solution developed for the Myntra HackerRamp 2026 challenge under **The Bharat Opportunity** theme.

The platform helps genuine new sellers overcome the **Cold-Start Trust Loop** by connecting them with experienced and trusted sellers who can mentor, recommend, and support them during onboarding.

By leveraging trusted seller networks instead of relying solely on reviews, the platform enables new sellers to gain visibility, credibility, and their first few orders faster. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Features

- 🤝 Seller Mentor Network for experienced sellers to mentor new sellers
- ✅ Trust-based onboarding through verified seller recommendations
- 📊 Mentor Dashboard to manage and monitor mentees
- 🚀 Seller Discovery Boost for recommended new sellers
- 📈 Performance monitoring using fulfilment, ratings, returns, and customer feedback
- 🌐 Scalable ecosystem that encourages collaboration among sellers :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

## Problem Statement

New sellers joining online marketplaces start with **zero sales and zero reviews**, making it difficult to gain buyer trust. As buyers naturally prefer established sellers, many genuine businesses struggle to get their initial orders and eventually leave the platform.

Our solution bridges this trust gap by enabling experienced sellers to mentor and recommend promising new sellers, helping them build credibility from day one. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
